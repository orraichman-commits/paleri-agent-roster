# Skill: Meta Ad Compliance — Copywriter

Practitioner heuristics aligned with Meta enforcement (not official policy text). A
rejected ad or a flagged account costs more than any conversion lift — compliance is part
of the craft, not a review afterthought. When policy risk and persuasion conflict, write
the compliant version and note the trade-off.

Canon for *what* Meta forbids, and for the niches PALERI will not advertise even with
compliant wording: `knowledge/memory/meta-ads-structure.md` and
`knowledge/memory/niches-to-avoid.md`. Official standards:
https://transparency.meta.com/he-il/policies/ad-standards

A denied niche is not fixed by softer copy. Skin, ingestibles, emergency gear, and
counterfeits do not get a "comfort language" version — they get no ad. Inside an allowed
niche, the traps below still apply. You do not advise opening a new ad account, appealing
a ban, switching cards, or using someone else's profile. A policy block is an escalation.

## The rejection traps (check every ad asset)
1. **Personal attributes** — never imply you know the reader's condition, weight, age,
   health, or finances. "אתה סובל מכאבי גב?" → rewrite to third person / general:
   "כאבי גב הם הסיוט של מי שיושב כל היום". Target the pain as a phenomenon, not the person.
2. **Medical/health claims** — no "מרפא", "מטפל ב", "מונע", no disease names attached to
   product promises (also a PALERI Hard Limit). Comfort/experience language instead.
3. **Before/after framing** — no transformation comparisons in copy ("מ-X ל-Y within…" on
   body/skin/hair). Describe the product experience, not the body change.
4. **Unrealistic results** — specific atypical outcomes presented as typical (income,
   weight, growth). Qualify or generalize.
5. **Sensational/clickbait withholding** — "לא תאמינו מה קרה אחר כך" style withholding,
   shock language, excessive caps/punctuation (!!!).
6. **Fake urgency/scarcity** — no invented countdowns or "נשארו 3 אחרונים" unless the offer
   is genuinely limited (truthful persuasion is a Hard Limit anyway).
7. **Negative self-image framing** — copy that makes the reader feel defective. Frame
   upward (what they gain), not downward (what's wrong with them).

## Procedure
- Scan every draft against the seven traps before returning it.
- A brief that *requires* a trap (e.g. a before/after promise) → refuse that element,
  provide the compliant alternative, and flag the conflict (see `operating-procedure.md`).
- Note in the draft's `Notes:` line which claims were softened/avoided and why — the
  reviewer needs to see compliance choices, not discover them.
