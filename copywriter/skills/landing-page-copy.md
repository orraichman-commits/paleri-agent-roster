# Skill: Product Page & Landing Copy — Copywriter

The page finishes what the ad started (Shopify product pages and pre-sell landers).
The visitor arrives warm from a specific ad — the page must continue *that* conversation:
same promise, same vocabulary, same awareness level. Ad-to-page mismatch is the silent killer.

## Section flow (in order — cut sections, don't shuffle them)
1. **Above the fold:** outcome headline (not product name), support line, primary CTA,
   hero visual reference. Decides staying-or-leaving in seconds.
2. **Trust strip:** ratings/order count/guarantee icons — someone else already trusts this.
   Israelis assume a scam until shown otherwise; front-load the trust.
3. **Problem resonance:** the pain in the customer's own words (from the Customer
   Intelligence Brief). Test: would the target reader nod?
4. **Mechanism:** why this works where alternatives failed — the "how" that makes the
   promise believable. Features appear here only translated to outcomes.
5. **Proof:** reviews with specifics, demonstration, numbers. Generic praise ("מוצר מעולה!")
   convinces no one — pick proof with concrete detail.
6. **Objection handling:** answer the top objections from the brief head-on (price →
   cost-per-use or comparison; trust → guarantee + return policy; "יעבוד לי?" → for-whom
   specificity; shipping worry → concrete days + tracking). FAQ format works.
7. **Closing CTA:** restate offer + risk reversal ("לא מתאים? מחזירים לכם הכל") + the CTA.

## CTA rules
Specific and low-friction: "בדקו זמינות במידה שלכם" / "הזמינו עכשיו — משלוח עד הבית" beat
"למידע נוסף". One CTA action per page, repeated — not competing actions.

## Product descriptions (short-format pages)
Compress the same flow: outcome line → 3-4 benefit bullets (So-What chain applied, concrete)
→ mini-proof → offer/CTA. Bullets state felt outcomes, not specs; specs go in a details
accordion for the Product Aware reader.

## Mobile discipline
Write for the phone: headline ≤ 2 lines on a narrow screen, paragraphs ≤ 3 lines, bullets
over prose. The desktop view is the edge case in Israeli eCommerce, not the norm.
