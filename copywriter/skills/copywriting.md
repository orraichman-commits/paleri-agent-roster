# Skill: Core Copywriting Craft — Copywriter

The craft that applies to every output type. Format-specific craft lives in
`hooks-and-ads.md`, `landing-page-copy.md`, and `messaging-copy.md`; Hebrew-language craft
in `hebrew-craft.md`; ad-policy discipline in `meta-compliance.md`.

## Awareness levels (Schwartz — match the copy to the reader's head)

| Level | Reader knows | Copy leads with |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | nothing relevant | story / pattern interrupt — never the product |
| Problem Aware | the pain, not the solutions | the pain, sharpened in their words |
| Solution Aware | solutions exist, not this product | the mechanism — why this way works |
| Product Aware | this product, unconvinced | proof, differentiation, offer |
| Most Aware | wants it | the deal — price, urgency, CTA |

The brief sets the level (PALERI mostly runs Problem Aware / Solution Aware). Writing
product-level copy at problem-level awareness is the most common way good products die in ads.

## The So-What chain (run on every claim)
Feature → functional benefit → felt outcome. "מטען מהיר 65W" → "טעינה מלאה בחצי שעה" →
"יוצאים מהבית בלי חרדת סוללה". Copy states the *last* link; the earlier links are support.
A claim that cannot survive three "אז מה?" questions does not belong in the copy.

## Specificity beats adjectives (non-negotiable)
- Numbers, timeframes, and concrete nouns over "מדהים/איכותי/מהפכני". "מתייבש תוך 4 דקות"
  sells; "ייבוש מהיר במיוחד" is noise.
- Odd, precise numbers read as measured; round ones read as invented.
- One idea per sentence. One promise per asset — the biggest one the evidence supports.
- Customer language over marketer language: reuse the words from the Customer Intelligence
  Brief (pains, objections, phrases) — that vocabulary IS the copy's raw material.

## Proof hierarchy (strongest available wins)
Specific customer result → aggregate numbers (orders, ratings) → demonstration ("watch it
X") → mechanism logic → naked assertion (avoid). Never fabricate proof — a missing proof
element is flagged `missing_upstream`, not invented (Hard Limit).

## Editing pass (after drafting, before returning)
1. Cut the first paragraph if the piece still works without it (it usually does — warm-up).
2. Delete every word that doesn't earn its place; shorten sentences until they're spoken.
3. Read aloud in Hebrew — anything you wouldn't say to a friend at a kiosk gets rewritten.
4. Check the promise ladder: headline promise = body promise = CTA promise. One broken rung
   kills conversion.
5. Run Self-Verification (`operating-procedure.md`) — human-sounding, compliant, on-brief.
