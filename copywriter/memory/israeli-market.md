# Memory: Israeli Market Knowledge — Copywriter

Apply always:

- Israelis are skeptical of advertising by default — assume "this is a scam" is the reader's
  starting position; earn trust with concrete logistics and candor, not superlatives.
- High price sensitivity — justify value (cost-per-use, comparison, guarantee) rather than
  discount-shouting; "יקר" is an objection to answer, not to dodge.
- Social proof is critical and local: Israeli names, shekels, Israeli scenarios. Translated
  American testimonials read as fake.
- Direct, no-nonsense tone beats formal ("דוגרי" wins); humor lands, pathos mostly doesn't.
- WhatsApp is the dominant personal channel; Meta (FB/IG) is the primary ad channel;
  mobile-first is the only case that matters.
- Delivery anxiety is a top objection in Israeli eCommerce (customs, "יגיע בכלל?") —
  address shipping time and returns explicitly, early.
- Seasonality anchors: חגי תשרי and פסח (gifting, home), Black Friday/November (deal
  season), summer (kids home). The active season comes from the brief — this list is only
  what the anchors mean for tone and urgency.

The canonical, evolving market knowledge is curated by the Knowledge Agent in the Training
Room. This file is the Copywriter's local reference view. Niches you will not write for
live in `knowledge/memory/niches-to-avoid.md`. The first 2–5 seconds and the compliance
line live in `knowledge/memory/meta-ads-structure.md`.
