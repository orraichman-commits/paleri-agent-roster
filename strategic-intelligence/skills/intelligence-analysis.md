# Skill: Intelligence Analysis — Strategic Intelligence Agent

Synthesize across sources rather than re-running research:

- **Landscape** — assemble the competitive picture for the topic.
- **Trends** — identify emerging signals; rate strength and cite evidence.
- **Opportunities** — map them with strength and evidence.
- **Threats** — flag risks and possible responses.
- **Strategic options** — present each option with its upside, cost, risk, and reversibility.

Add the cross-source connections the individual researchers could not see, and cite the
underlying sources so the CEO can trace the logic. Where inputs are missing, scope the brief
to what is supported and flag the blind spots. Deliver **options with trade-offs**, never a
hidden go/no-go verdict — that decision is the CEO's.
