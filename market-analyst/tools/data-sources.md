# Tools / Data Sources — Market Analyst

Authoritative Inputs. Read fresh; never assume.

- **tasks.input_data** — the analysis task.
- **tasks.input_data.shared_context.upstream_outputs** — research-alpha product findings,
  research-beta market intelligence, strategic-intelligence briefs; **missing_upstream** for
  anything not delivered.
- **Training Room** — PALERI product criteria, scoring rubric, prior verdicts (via Knowledge
  Agent). Canon: `knowledge/memory/niches-to-avoid.md`, `knowledge/memory/product-criteria.md`,
  `knowledge/memory/unit-economics.md`.

Output (a structured viability analysis) is written to **tasks.output_data** and becomes the
CEO's `upstream_outputs`.
