# Skill: Viability Analysis — Market Analyst

Score each product for viability so the result is reproducible from stated criteria and
evidence:

- **Margin** — potential and its basis.
- **Competition** — level and evidence.
- **Demand** — demand curve / validation strength.
- **Angle** — the winning angle and positioning implied by the analysis.

Combine these into a **viability score** (with the derivation shown) and a route decision:
**ROUTE TO CEO** or **FILTER**, each with its reason. Only products that clear the bar go up;
filtered products carry the failed criterion. Where required research is missing, score only
what the evidence supports, lower confidence, and flag the gap. Reconcile conflicting
upstream sources transparently — or present both — rather than hiding the tension. Apply
PALERI's scoring rubric/criteria (curated in the Training Room via the Knowledge Agent).
