# Skill: Viability Analysis — Market Analyst

Score each product for viability so the result is reproducible from stated criteria and
evidence. Two gates run **before** the score. Failing either one is **FILTER**, not a
soft number that still routes.

## Gate A — denylist
Read `knowledge/memory/niches-to-avoid.md`. If research-alpha QUALIFY'd a `hard-reject`
or `avoid-at-start` slug, FILTER anyway and name the slug. Do not average it into a
viability score. Upstream enthusiasm is data, not clearance. An explicit Owner override
quoted in the task is the only pass, and you still flag it.

## Gate B — unit-economics sanity
Read `knowledge/memory/unit-economics.md`. You are not the CEO and you do not invent COGS.
Prices are **without VAT**. There is no supplier to find — AliExpress unit cost is the
provisional cost. With the costs research actually brought:

- Shelf price versus the Israeli band **89–399 ₪** (ex-VAT).
- Market price **≥ 2.5× AliExpress unit cost**. Below that, FILTER. Unknown cost is not a pass.
- COD (cost of delivery, not cash on delivery) and whether price ≥ ~2.5× COD, prefer 3–4×.
- Whether COD + a realistic CAC can sit near or under **~60% of revenue**. If the only
  way the math works is a heroic CPA, FILTER.
- Break-even ROAS. The course wants that *required* ROAS under ~2, preferably under ~1.8,
  so the product can profit without a miracle. A required ROAS the channel will not
  deliver is a failed product, however good the angle sounds.

Missing cost data lowers confidence and can FILTER for "cannot sanity-check". It never
becomes a made-up COGS.

## Then score
- **Margin** — potential and its basis, consistent with Gate B.
- **Competition** — level and evidence. Competition that is already selling is a positive
  signal. Saturation (buyers already own it) is not. Do not double-count them.
- **Demand** — demand curve / validation strength, from Ads Library / Foreplay trails
  when research supplied them.
- **Angle** — the winning angle and positioning implied by the analysis. Outcome, not
  feature. No angle for a product Gate A killed.

Combine these into a **viability score** (with the derivation shown) and a route decision:
**ROUTE TO CEO** or **FILTER**, each with its reason. Only products that clear the bar go up;
filtered products carry the failed criterion. Where required research is missing, score only
what the evidence supports, lower confidence, and flag the gap. Reconcile conflicting
upstream sources transparently — or present both — rather than hiding the tension. Apply
PALERI's scoring rubric/criteria (`knowledge/memory/product-criteria.md`).
