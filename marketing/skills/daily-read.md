# Skill: Daily Read — Marketing

Read a live campaign every 24 hours. Recommend. Or changes the account.

## Start condition
Or has confirmed the launch and given the **campaign name and ID**. Without that ID,
do not guess which campaign is ours. Say the read is blocked.

## What decides, and what only explains
The decision pair is **ROAS + spend**, read against the product's break-even ROAS.

Break-even ROAS comes from the CEO's products table:

`margin = price − cost − 5% clearing` · `break-even ROAS = price / margin`

Price is without VAT. If the row still carries the AliExpress cost because LIO has
not returned a supplier quote, say the break-even is provisional and do not kill on it.

**CPA, CTR, CPC, and frequency diagnose.** They explain a weak or winning ad set.
They do not set the label and they do not justify a kill on their own.

## Label every ad set
- **Winning** — ROAS is above break-even, and spend is large enough to trust the
  ratio. Tiny spend and a flattering ROAS is not winning.
- **Waiting** — too little spend or too little time to call it. Waiting is not weak.
- **Weak** — spend is real and ROAS is not holding above break-even.

## Moves you may recommend
- **+20%** for each additional **48 hours** that an ad set stays winning. Each
  extra 48 hours of success is another +20% recommendation, not a single raise.
  A raise wakes `finance-controller`. Finance checks increases only.
- **−20%** when the ad set is weak. Finance does not re-review a cut.
- **Kill** only after **48–72 hours** with ROAS below that product's break-even
  ROAS. Earlier than 48 hours, the label is waiting or weak, not dead. A kill is
  a recommendation. Or turns the ad set off.

Do not recommend a raise, a cut, and a kill on the same ad set in the same read.

## Fatigue and scale
- **Creative fatigue** (frequency climbing while ROAS falls, and the ads are the
  same ones that already won) goes back to `creative-strategist`. That round must
  pass **Gate 2** again. You do not brief a replacement.
- **CBO-ready** means an ABO ad set has won on ROAS + spend long enough to leave
  the test. Recommend a CBO (Advantage Campaign Budget) scale home for that winner.
  Do not rebuild the test as CBO. ASC is not the next step from a first test.

## Handoff of the read
Wake the CEO with the labels and the recommended moves, so Or gets a **short daily
message**, not a deck. A NotebookLM deck is for the end of the test, not for this
read. Wake Finance only for a recommended increase. Compare nothing to a monthly
Meta ceiling — there isn't one. Approval is per campaign.
