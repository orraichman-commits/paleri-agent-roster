# Skill: ABO Test Structure — Marketing

Build the pre-publish test. You recommend structure and a budget. You do not create
the campaign. Structure rules live in `knowledge/memory/meta-ads-structure.md`; this
skill is how you apply them to one product.

## When you run
`shopify` has a draft (prices **without VAT**) and the creative chain has assets.
If either is missing, send the package back. Do not invent ads to fill a grid.

## How to group
One ABO test. Budget sits on the ad set, not on the campaign.

Group the assets into ad sets by the thing you are actually testing:

- **Angle** — one angle per ad set when the angles differ.
- **Avatar** — do not mix avatars inside an ad set just to save a row.
- **Copy** — hooks and primary text that make different promises are different ads,
  and different promises that should not share delivery are different ad sets.
- **Hook** — the first 2–3 seconds (or the first line) is the hook under test.

Inside one ad set: no more than **5–6 ads** when the daily budget is a few hundred
shekels, and no more than **3** when it is around 50–60 ₪. Do not mix image and video
in the same ad set. That mix is an ASC scale move, and this is not scale.

Campaign type for the test: **Sales**. Not Advantage+ and not CBO. CBO is what you
recommend later, for a winner, after the test.

## Budget you propose
State both layers. Do not collapse them into one number when they disagree.

1. **Per ad set, per day.** Product up to about 400 ₪ → **20–35 ₪**. Above that →
   **60–100 ₪**.
2. **Total daily test budget.** At least **0.5×** the final product price. Ideal is
   **100–200%** of that price.

The final price is the ex-VAT shelf price on the Shopify draft and on the CEO's
products table. If those two differ, stop and name the conflict. Do not average them.

Hand the plan to `finance-controller`. Finance judges whether it is reasonable, not
excessive, and consistent with canon (including COD+CAC ≤ ~60% and break-even ROAS
below 2). Their opinion goes to the CEO for Gate 2. You do not treat your proposal
as approved spend.

## What you do not do here
- Do not open Ads Manager, do not publish, do not set a live budget.
- Do not move the test to CBO "so the algorithm can pick."
- Do not drop an ad because its hook feels weak. That judgment already happened in
  Creative. You organize what passed.
