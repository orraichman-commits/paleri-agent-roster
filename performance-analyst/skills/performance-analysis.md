# Skill: Performance Analysis — Performance Analyst

Turn numbers into prioritized, actionable insight:

- **Campaign KPIs** — track ROAS, CTR, CPA, AOV with their time windows.
- **Operational KPIs** — monitor cross-office performance and throughput.
- **Bottlenecks** — locate underperforming areas and quantify their impact.
- **Prioritization** — surface only what materially moves KPIs; don't bury the CEO in data.
- **Anomalies** — flag budget anomalies / spend spikes to the Finance Office (and the AI Cost
  Manager when the spike is AI spend).

Distinguish measured facts from inference and label estimates. When a connector or dataset is
unavailable, state the coverage limit rather than extrapolating as fact.
