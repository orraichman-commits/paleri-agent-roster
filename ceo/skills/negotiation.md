# Skill: Negotiation — CEO (suppliers & service providers)

Dropshipping margin is made in sourcing: shaving product cost, shipping time, or payment
terms moves contribution directly (see `unit-economics.md`). The CEO prepares the strategy
and drafts the messages; the **Owner sends them and signs** — any terms involving real money
are Owner-approved, always (Hard Limit).

## Preparation (before any offer or counter)
1. **Total landed cost, not unit price.** Product + shipping + defect/refund rate + payment
   terms + delivery speed. A "cheaper" supplier with 21-day shipping and 8% defects is more
   expensive — run both offers through the contribution equation before comparing.
2. **BATNA.** Name the concrete alternative: a second sourced quote, or staying with the
   current supplier. Leverage = willingness to walk. No alternative sourced yet → get one
   first (delegate to Research), then negotiate.
3. **Walk-away number.** Written before the exchange, derived from break-even math — never
   decided mid-conversation.
4. **Their interests.** Suppliers want steady volume, low support cost, prompt payment.
   Trade what is cheap for PALERI and valuable to them.

## Tactics (message-drafting rules)
- **Anchor first** when the market price is known; counter with precise, non-round numbers
  (a $23.40 counter reads as calculated; $25 reads as movable).
- **Never split the difference by default** — it rewards the more aggressive anchor.
- **Trade, don't concede.** Every concession is priced and exchanged: volume commitment ↔
  payment after fulfillment, faster shipping line, lower MOQ, better defect policy. List
  the asks and the gives before drafting.
- **Calibrated questions over demands:** "How can we get to X?" / "What flexibility is there
  on payment terms?" — extracts information and applies pressure without an ultimatum.
- **Don't bid against yourself.** One offer, then silence; a real deadline stated once beats
  repeated pressure.
- **Everything in writing**, and verify before scaling: order samples, measure actual
  shipping time — supplier promises are marketing until measured.

## Output form
A negotiation brief: goal, BATNA, walk-away number, opening anchor, the concession trade
list (asks ↔ gives), a drafted opening message in the counterpart's language, and the
explicit point where the Owner decides.
