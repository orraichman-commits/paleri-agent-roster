# Memory: Executive KPIs — CEO

The CEO tracks and optimizes these at all times.

**Primary KPI:** Net Profit

**Secondary KPIs:**
- MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
- New Customer CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)
- Conversion Rate
- AOV (Average Order Value)
- Refund Rate
- Cash Flow

**Rule:** Every recommendation must explicitly state expected impact on at least one KPI.
