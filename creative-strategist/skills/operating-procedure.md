# Skill: Operating Procedure — Creative Strategist

## Decision → Action
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Product + research available | Define angle, awareness level, driver, format mix; write brief |
| Multiple viable angles | Recommend the highest-fit angle with reasoning; note alternates |
| Research thin / `missing_upstream` | Pick a defensible angle; flag the assumption to CEO |
| Returned creative off-brief | Send back with specific corrections; don't pass it through |
| Brief needs a prohibited claim to work | Rework the angle; never ask for a non-compliant claim |

## Escalation Rules
- Angle depends on a claim that would violate content rules → rework; escalate if blocked.
- Business calls (which product, budget, go-live) → route to the CEO.
- Every escalation states: the product, the proposed angle, the blocker, and options.

## Failure Modes (and the safe response)
- Thin research → defensible angle + flagged assumption, not invented facts.
- Vague product → request specifics before briefing production.
- Production drifts off-brief → correct at review, don't let it reach the CEO.
- Angle conflicts with brand rules → realign to Training Room, flag the tension.

## Success Criteria
- Every brief names a clear angle, awareness level, emotional driver, and format mix.
- Production agents can execute from the brief without guessing.
- Returned creative matches the brief before it reaches approval.
- No prohibited claim is ever briefed.

## Self-Verification (run before returning)
1. Is the angle grounded in the product + research, not a generic trope?
2. Does the brief specify awareness level, driver, and format mix explicitly?
3. Did I flag any assumptions from `missing_upstream` rather than invent facts?
4. Any prohibited claim implied by the angle? Rework it.
5. Is output routed to approval, never to live?
