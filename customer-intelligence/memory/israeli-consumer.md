# Memory: Israeli Consumer Psychology — Durable Reference

Stable knowledge for customer analysis in the Israeli dropshipping market.
(The Knowledge Agent's Training Room is canonical; this is the agent-local view.
Update when performance data or new research contradicts it — don't let it fossilize.)

Do not build this picture for a denied niche (`knowledge/memory/niches-to-avoid.md`).
Motivations are LF8 outcomes (`knowledge/memory/product-criteria.md`), not a feature list.
Foreplay / Ads Library notes, when research supplied them, calibrate tone and length only.

## Baseline traits
- **Skepticism is the default.** Israelis assume advertising exaggerates. Trust must be
  earned inside the first seconds — social proof, specificity, and directness beat polish.
- **Price sensitivity is high, but value beats cheap.** "שווה את זה" (worth it) is the
  bar — justify value; a too-cheap price triggers quality suspicion.
- **Directness converts.** Formal or corporate tone reads as foreign/fake. The winning
  register is the way a friend recommends a product: "תקשיבי, זה פשוט עובד".
- **Social proof is near-mandatory.** "כמה כבר קנו", reviews, real usage photos.
  An unproven product carries the "עוד מוצר מאליאקספרס" objection by default.
- **Mobile-first, Meta-primary.** Discovery happens in the Facebook/Instagram feed on a
  phone, mostly evenings; attention is measured in seconds.
- **WhatsApp is the trust channel.** Willingness to message a business on WhatsApp is
  high; a WhatsApp path lowers purchase anxiety.

## Standard objection set (apply per product, rank by kill-power)
1. Shipping time — "מתי זה באמת יגיע?" (chronic AliExpress trauma).
2. Authenticity/quality — "זה נראה טוב בתמונה, במציאות זה זבל".
3. Post-purchase support — "אם יש בעיה, אין עם מי לדבר".
4. Price justification — "אפשר למצוא את זה בחצי מחיר".
5. Social validation — "לא מכיר אף אחד שקנה מהאתר הזה".

## Buying-mode heuristics
- Low ticket (<₪100) + high emotion → impulse mode; friction is the enemy.
- Mid ticket (₪100–₪300) → needs one strong proof element + risk reversal.
- Family/child/home-safety products → the buyer is often buying identity ("הורה טוב"),
  not the object; identity pain outranks functional pain.
- Holiday/seasonal windows (חגים, קיץ, חזרה לבית ספר) reshape urgency and gift framing.

## Segmentation notes
- Center vs periphery: delivery expectations and pickup-point habits differ.
- Age bands behave differently on Meta: 25–34 story/reel-native; 45+ feed + longer copy,
  higher WhatsApp usage, stronger support objection.
- Hebrew is the default language of trust; English-mixed copy reads as less local.

## Anti-patterns (learned)
- Overpromising triggers the skepticism reflex harder than saying nothing.
- Hard-sell urgency ("רק היום!!!") is pattern-matched as scam unless backed by a real
  mechanism.
- Generic lifestyle imagery without the product in real Israeli context underperforms.
